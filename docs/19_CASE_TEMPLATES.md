# 19 — Initial investigation templates

Templates are structured prompts/checklists, never permission to perform intrusive collection. Each inherits scope/authority gate and requires case-specific supervisor review.

## A. Procurement / supplier conflict review

Questions: relationship between suppliers; contract/invoice duplication; corporate registry facts; dates and payments that require reconciliation. Intake: commissioning authority, datasets, legal basis, source terms. Outputs: source inventory, relationship graph with citations, invoice timeline, unverified hypotheses, approved findings and limitations.

## B. Insurance incident chronology

Questions: what occurred and when; which accounts are consistent; what observations are independently supported. Inputs: incident brief, statements, photographs and permissioned records. Outputs: chronology with time uncertainties, disputed facts, selected exhibits, report with explicit gaps. No automatic fraud accusation.

## C. Workplace/internal investigations

Question: allegations and evidence scope as defined by commissioning employer and legal advice. Required additional checks: employment privacy, monitoring guidance, conflict of interest, witness confidentiality, minimisation. Outputs: segregated statements, allegation/evidence cross-reference, reviewed outcome draft; no general employee monitoring.

## D. Litigation support document review

Questions: key chronology and documentary support; contradictions between statements; exhibit index. Inputs: counsel-provided data within confidentiality/privilege boundary. Outputs: citation-backed chronology, neutral report draft, privilege review checklist and secure disclosure package.

## Shared report structure

1. Instruction and scope, with redacted mandate details where appropriate.
2. Investigative methodology and sources, acquisition limitations.
3. Chronology of verified observations with uncertainty.
4. Findings, each linked to supporting/contrary exhibits.
5. Alternative explanations, limitations and unanswered questions.
6. Conclusions constrained by the evidence; no unsupported guilt declarations.
7. Exhibits, custody/integrity appendix and reviewer sign-off.

Case templates must offer data retention and explicit client-release choices. Do not automatically publish internal hypotheses.
