## Execution rules and evidence

Own routine reversible design and debugging. Each coherent draft PR includes code, meaningful tests, reproduction instructions and an exact handoff. Record implementation and actual API/database/browser/worker/device/offline/restore results separately. Reuse passing completed slices; do not repeatedly acquire project names or rebuild foundation functionality. Work around a local execution blocker by advancing an independent authorised item, never by weakening isolation. Keep third-party research copies ignored and do not redistribute unapproved artifacts.

No percentages substitute for exit criteria. Every completed row must link the exact tested revision and evidence; partial completion leaves its remaining criteria open. The repository's full work packages remain authoritative for broader scope. After all authorised requirements and relevant tests pass, assess repository backlog priorities, then pause recurring automation; user-dependent legal/pilot/provider decisions remain explicitly outstanding.
