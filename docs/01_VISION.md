# 01 — Vision, positioning and users

## Mission

Build the operating system that takes a professional investigation **from authorised instruction to defensible, evidence-referenced explanation**. A single case model joins instructions, assignments, field observations, documents, entities, hypotheses, analyst review, approved findings and controlled delivery.

## Product thesis

Investigators often shuttle between spreadsheets, chat, voice recorders, hard drives, OSINT utilities, research documents and word processors. INVEOS should reduce re-entry, gaps in provenance, evidence loss, inconsistent timelines and delayed reporting. AI should eliminate clerical work without replacing investigators' judgement.

## Positioning

- Public-facing name: **INVEOS — Investigation Operating System**. Working name only; trademark, domain and brand clearance pending.
- Owner/brand: Black State Group. Distinct from the NOIR security operations programme. Potential shared modules are subject to architecture, licence and access review.
- Product surfaces: Command (desktop), Field (mobile), Client (release portal).
- Principles: case-centred; evidence-led; human-led; offline-capable; multi-tenant; privacy-first; provenance-first.

## Ideal initial customers

1. **Small UK investigation agency (2–20 investigators):** daily cases, field journals, evidence collation, client reporting, agency coordination.
2. **Solo professional investigator:** rapid intake and reports without enterprise complexity.
3. **Corporate investigations and insurance teams:** document-heavy case review and access governance.
4. **Solicitors/litigation support:** evidence bundles, chronology and exhibit traceability.
5. **Security incident review teams:** incidents, media evidence, statement consolidation, only within lawful authority.

## Main jobs-to-be-done

- Know what to do on this case next.
- Record what I observed without losing chronology or originals.
- Establish which claim comes from which source, with uncertainties visible.
- Locate facts and contradictions across large volumes of material.
- Produce a reliable deliverable, with reviewed exhibits and a verifiable archive.
- Give the client only what they are permitted to see.
- Account for work, deadlines and costs without switching tools.

## Intended differentiation

One complete record lineage: **instruction → lawful acquisition → original → analysis → reviewable assertion → approved finding → report citation → release**. Every transition can be traced, audited and reversed through corrections without falsifying history.

## Explicit non-goals for first release

- Mass profiling or unbounded personal dossiers; covert GPS tracking; harvesting private accounts; bypassing sites' access controls; interception of communications; facial recognition across public imagery.
- Promising admissibility, forensic certification or an automated truth verdict.
- Replacing specialist digital forensics suites or lawful counsel.
- A broad intelligence platform before core case capture/reporting actually works.

## Success definition

Five pilot teams complete representative cases entirely in INVEOS, including disconnected field capture and a client release, with **zero lost originals**, all published claims source-linked, role isolation verified, meaningful time savings measured and positive pilot feedback. No such outcome has yet been verified.
